01

Sense.

The ESP32 reads two sensors and publishes their values to Firebase.

DHT11 TEMPERATURE · HUMIDITY

Measures air temperature in degrees Celsius and relative humidity in percent. A single digital reading, published continuously in real time.

LDR AMBIENT LIGHT

An analog light-dependent resistor reports ambient brightness on a 0–1023 scale. This value is what drives automatic control.

02

Connect.

The device and the interface meet in a single live source of truth.

ESP32 Wi-Fi

An ESP32 microcontroller joins the local network and maintains the link between the physical world and the service layer.

Firebase REALTIME DATABASE

Sensor readings, operating mode and appliance state live in Firebase. Every listener — the dashboard and the device — sees changes the instant they land.

03

Decide.

Control happens two ways — you decide, or the environment decides.

Manual ON · OFF

From the dashboard you send an explicit command. The device carries it to the relay and the relay switches the lamp.

Automatic LDR < THRESHOLD

Below a configured light threshold the lamp turns on; at or above it, the lamp turns off. The rule is evaluated continuously from live readings.

04 · Act

Control the physical world.

Relay GPIO SWITCH

The ESP32 drives a relay module that opens and closes the mains circuit feeding a 230 V bulb.

Smart bulb 230 V

A lamp becomes addressable: on, off, or governed by the light it sits in.

DARK → ON   BRIGHT → OFF
System Architecture

One path. Two directions.

Readings travel upward from the sensors to the dashboard. Commands travel back down to the actuator. Firebase is the single point every component reads from and writes to.
